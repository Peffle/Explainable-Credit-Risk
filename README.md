# Explainable-Credit-Risk

## The problem
Every decision that is accepted from AI, deserve explanation. Many banks, naturally effect most of the people which have accounts in them. If we rely on AI to make every decision for us, the manipulation can be in the blink of an eye, while we don't have time to react then. If AI system or model is telling the truth, we must understand weather that is correct truly or just for now, (does it last?), (how accurate it is?) and what could possibly go wrong if it isn't? the problem we are trying to solve is "If AI system or model is generatating innacurate decisions or explanations which contain informations of decision we must be ready and prepared before that even happens." Everything can happen, therefore we are bringing a possible solution to it, to improve not only banks and their internal systems, but to help and save humans from being manipulated or possibly lied on AI decision on handing the loans to one human or not for someone is a good or correct thing to do and why and why not.


## The question
Can banks trust AI-generated explanations of credit decisions?

## The plan
1. Build and understand a credit risk model (German Credit dataset)
2. Move to realistic data and rigorous explanations (SHAP)
3. Evaluate whether AI-generated explanations are faithful/accurate to the model
